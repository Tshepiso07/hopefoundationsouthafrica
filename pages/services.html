01 / NOURISH

Food support

The proposed food support programme would coordinate community food drives and essential-goods collections. Supporters could contribute suitable non-perishable food items or help with sorting and distribution through verified community partners.

  • Non-perishable food collection drives.
  • Volunteer support for packing and sorting.
  • Partner referrals based on community needs.

02 / EMPOWER

Learning support

The learning support concept aims to encourage young people through reading activities, homework assistance and access to donated educational materials. Activities would be planned with parents, educators and suitable partners.

  • Reading and homework club support.
  • Stationery and book collection drives.
  • Volunteer tutoring subject to screening.
Enquire about learning support →
03 / CONNECT

Community action

Community action would bring residents, local groups and sponsors together around practical projects such as clean-up days, donation collections and skills-sharing events.

  • Community clean-up and action days.
  • Skills-sharing and volunteer opportunities.
  • Local business and sponsor partnerships.
